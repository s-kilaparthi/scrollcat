## Detailed Description

**Never let a message go unanswered again.**

ScrollCat is your AI-powered reply assistant that lives right inside your browser. A friendly floating cat watches your conversations on WhatsApp Web, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, Gmail, Slack, and Twitter — and suggests three perfect replies the moment you need them.

**How it works**

1. Install ScrollCat and visit any supported messaging site
2. A floating 🐱 cat appears in the corner with a platform badge (💬 for WhatsApp, 📱 for Instagram, ✈️ for Telegram)
3. Focus any reply box or tap the cat — ScrollCat reads the last message and generates 3 contextual reply chips
4. Click a chip — your reply is inserted instantly. No copy-paste, no typing, no tab-switching

**Key features**

- **Smart AI replies** — Three contextual suggestions for every message, tuned to your tone (Friendly, Casual, or Professional)
- **Works everywhere** — WhatsApp Web, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, Telegram Web, Gmail, Slack, and Twitter/X
- **Platform-aware defaults** — Even without an API key, ScrollCat returns smart per-site defaults (e.g. "Thank you so much! 🙏" on Instagram)
- **Optional Claude AI** — Bring your own Anthropic API key for even smarter, more personalized suggestions
- **Creator & Business modes** — Set your user type so replies match your voice as an influencer or business owner
- **Per-site toggles** — Enable or disable ScrollCat on any platform individually
- **Keyboard shortcut** — Alt+R toggles the suggestion panel instantly
- **Daily stats** — Track how many replies you suggested and used, broken down by platform

**Privacy first**

ScrollCat processes messages locally in your browser. We do not operate servers. We do not store your conversations. Your Claude API key is saved only in Chrome's local storage on your device and sent only to Anthropic when you use it. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

**Who is it for?**

- **Content creators & influencers** — Reply to fan DMs, collab requests, and rate card inquiries in seconds without breaking your creative flow
- **Small business owners** — Answer customer questions on WhatsApp and Instagram instantly, even while you're busy
- **Professionals** — Keep up with Gmail and Slack without spending hours drafting replies

**Getting started**

1. Click "Add to Chrome"
2. Visit web.whatsapp.com, instagram.com, or any supported site
3. Tap the cat or focus a reply box — suggestions appear automatically
4. (Optional) Open the popup, add your Claude API key, and choose your tone

**Pricing**

ScrollCat is free to use with smart per-site defaults. Connect your own Claude API key for premium AI-powered suggestions. The Android app offers Creator ($4.99/mo) and Business ($9.99/mo) plans with unlimited on-device AI replies.
