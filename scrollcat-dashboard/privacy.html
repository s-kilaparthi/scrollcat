🐱 ScrollCat Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Our Promise

ScrollCat is built to be private by design. We do not operate servers that store your messages, contacts, or conversations. Your data stays on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing is collected on our servers. ScrollCat has no backend. All AI processing happens on your device using Google ML Kit (Gemini Nano and Smart Reply). Your messages are never uploaded to ScrollCat.

Locally on your device, ScrollCat may store:

  • Your app settings (reply tone, cat size, auto-reply rules)
  • Daily usage counters (replies used today) to enforce free-tier limits
  • Your Claude API key, encrypted, if you choose to provide one

Notification Access

ScrollCat reads incoming notification content (sender name and message text) solely to generate reply suggestions and send replies through each app's own reply mechanism. Notification content is held in memory only long enough to generate a suggestion and is never persisted to disk or transmitted to any server.

Accessibility Service

The accessibility service is used only for gesture control — scrolling, back button, and volume — when you interact with the floating cat. ScrollCat does not read passwords, banking apps, or sensitive form fields. It does not log or transmit accessibility data.

Claude API (Optional)

If you provide your own Anthropic Claude API key, the message you are replying to is sent to Anthropic's servers (api.anthropic.com) to generate suggestions, under Anthropic's privacy policy. This is entirely opt-in. Without an API key, all processing stays on your device.

Chrome Extension

The ScrollCat Chrome extension reads the current conversation on supported sites (WhatsApp Web, Instagram, etc.) to generate reply suggestions. Your API key is stored locally in Chrome storage. No data is sent to ScrollCat servers.

Third-Party Services

  • Google ML Kit — on-device AI for reply suggestions
  • Google Play Billing — subscription payments processed by Google
  • Anthropic API — optional, user-provided API key only

Your Choices

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings or Chrome extension settings. Uninstalling the app or extension deletes all locally stored data.

Children

ScrollCat is not directed at children under 13.
